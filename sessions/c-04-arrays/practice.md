# Practice: Arrays and strings

### 4.1 Print the list ⏱️ 6 min

Store five `int` scores. Print them on separate lines with a `for` loop.

### 4.2 Average ⏱️ 8 min

Add those five scores and print the average as a `double`.

### 4.3 Largest ⏱️ 8 min

Write `int largest(int a[], int n)` that returns the biggest value. Pass the array and its length.

### 4.4 Count letters ⏱️ 10 min

Store `char word[] = "wizard"`. Count characters until `'\0'` and print the length. Do not use `strlen` yet. Then check your answer with `strlen`.
